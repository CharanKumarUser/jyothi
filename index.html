Clinical cases

Decisions under pressure

Representative cases from Government General Hospital, Kadapa.

Acute inferior wall STEMI, 54-year-old patient

Presentation

Crushing chest pain radiating to the left arm, sweating and BP 84/50 mmHg. ECG within 4 minutes: ST elevation in II, III and aVF.

Management

Aspirin 300 mg + Clopidogrel 300 mg loading, nitrates withheld (suspected RV infarct), oxygen, careful fluid challenge and urgent transfer for catheterisation.

Diabetic ketoacidosis with sepsis in known T2DM

Presentation

Altered sensorium, Kussmaul breathing, RBS 480 mg/dL, pH 7.12, bicarbonate 9 mEq/L and urine ketones 3+.

Management

Normal-saline resuscitation, insulin infusion at 0.1 U/kg/hr, hourly potassium replacement, serial ABGs and empiric sepsis cover.

Primigravida at 39 weeks with meconium-stained liquor

Presentation

Active labour at 8 cm with thick meconium-stained fluid; CTG showed late decelerations (fetal distress).

Management

Expedited delivery with mediolateral episiotomy, neonatal suctioning (APGAR 8/10 at 5 min), AMTSL with oxytocin and two-layer repair.

Complex forearm traumatic laceration

Presentation

10 cm jagged volar forearm laceration from farm machinery; haemostasis secured, nerves and vessels intact.

Management

Pressure irrigation, debridement, layered closure (3-0 polyglactin, 4-0 nylon mattress sutures), tetanus toxoid and antibiotics.
