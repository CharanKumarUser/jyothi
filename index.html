06 · Case Files

Decisions made under pressure.

Representative scenarios from Government General Hospital, Kadapa. Open a file to read the protocol.

CASE 01 Casualty Resuscitation Acute inferior wall STEMI in a 54-year-old patient
Presentation

Crushing retrosternal chest pain radiating to the left arm, diaphoresis and hypotension (BP 84/50 mmHg). A 12-lead ECG within 4 minutes showed ST elevation in II, III and aVF with reciprocal depressions.

Protocol executed

Dual antiplatelet loading (Aspirin 300 mg + Clopidogrel 300 mg), nitrates withheld on suspicion of RV infarction, high-flow O₂, gentle isotonic fluid challenge and coordinated transfer for emergency catheterisation.

CASE 02 ICU Metabolic Emergency Diabetic ketoacidosis with sepsis in known T2DM
Presentation

Altered sensorium, Kussmaul breathing, RBS 480 mg/dL, arterial pH 7.12, bicarbonate 9 mEq/L and urine ketones 3+, confirming high anion-gap metabolic acidosis.

Protocol executed

Aggressive normal-saline resuscitation, IV regular insulin infusion at 0.1 U/kg/hr, hourly potassium replacement, serial ABG monitoring and broad-spectrum empiric sepsis cover.

CASE 03 Labour Room Emergency Primigravida at 39 weeks with meconium-stained liquor
Presentation

Admitted in active labour at 8 cm dilation with thick meconium-stained fluid; CTG showed late decelerations indicating fetal distress.

Protocol executed

Expedited delivery with right mediolateral episiotomy, immediate neonatal suctioning, APGAR 8/10 at 5 minutes, AMTSL with prophylactic oxytocin and two-layer perineal repair.

CASE 04 Emergency Minor Surgery Complex forearm traumatic laceration
Presentation

An agricultural worker with a jagged 10 cm volar forearm laceration from farm machinery. Haemostasis secured; neurovascular examination documented intact peripheral nerve function.

Protocol executed

Pulsatile saline irrigation, selective debridement, layered closure with 3-0 polyglactin dermal and 4-0 nylon mattress sutures, tetanus toxoid and antibiotic cover.
