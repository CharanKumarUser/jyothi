Clinical Decision-Making & Patient Outcomes

Representative medical scenarios highlighting evidence-based clinical protocols, rapid stabilization, and diagnostic acumen.

Casualty Resuscitation Govt General Hospital, Kadapa

Acute Inferior Wall STEMI in a 54-Year-Old Patient

Patient presented with crushing retrosternal chest pain radiating to left arm, diaphoresis, and hypotension (BP 84/50 mmHg). Immediate 12-lead ECG obtained within 4 minutes revealed ST-segment elevations in leads II, III, and aVF with reciprocal depressions.

Clinical Protocol Executed:
Golden-hour dual antiplatelet loading (Aspirin 300mg + Clopidogrel 300mg), sublingual nitroglycerin avoided due to RV infarction suspicion, high-flow O2, gentle isotonic fluid challenge, and coordinated emergency cardiology catheterization transfer.
ICU Metabolic Emergency Govt General Hospital, Kadapa

Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA) with Sepsis in Known T2DM

Patient presented with altered sensorium, Kussmaul breathing, random blood sugar 480 mg/dL, arterial pH 7.12, serum bicarbonate 9 mEq/L, and elevated urine ketones (3+). High anion gap metabolic acidosis confirmed.

Clinical Protocol Executed:
Aggressive volume resuscitation with normal saline, continuous IV regular insulin infusion (0.1 units/kg/hr) titrated to glycemic response, strict hourly potassium replacement, serial ABG monitoring, and broad-spectrum empiric sepsis cover.
Labor Room Emergency Govt General Hospital, Kadapa

Primigravida at 39 Weeks with Meconium-Stained Liquor

Full-term primigravida admitted in active stage of labor with cervical dilation 8cm and thick meconium stained amniotic fluid. Cardiotocography demonstrated late decelerations indicating fetal distress.

Clinical Protocol Executed:
Expedited delivery assisted with right mediolateral episiotomy; immediate neonatal suctioning, APGAR assessment (8/10 at 5 mins), prophylactic oxytocin for active third stage management (AMTSL), and two-layer anatomical perineal reconstruction.
Emergency Minor Surgery Govt General Hospital, Kadapa

Complex Forearm Traumatic Laceration

Young agricultural worker presented with a jagged 10cm laceration over the volar forearm sustained in farm machinery. Hemostasis secured; motor and sensory neurovascular examinations documented intact peripheral nerve function.

Clinical Protocol Executed:
Copious pulsatile saline irrigation, selective devitalized tissue debridement, anatomical layered wound closure with 3-0 polyglactin dermal sutures and 4-0 non-absorbable nylon monofilament mattress sutures, tetanus toxoid toxoid, and antibiotics.
